Hamare Karyakshetra

Focus Sectors

Zoon Charitable Trust samaj ke teen sabse ahem kshetron mein musalsal sargarmi (activity) se kaam kar rahi hai:

Education (Taleem)

Humara maqsad hai ki koi bhi bacha paise ki tangi ki wajah se taleem se mehroom na rahe. Iske liye hum nishulk shiksha ke saath buniyadi cheezein distribute karte hain.

  • Gareeb & yateem bachon ki school fees support
  • Bags, Books aur Stationery distribution
  • Career counseling camps gaon-dehat mein

Healthcare (Sehat)

ZCT rural areas aur gareeb bastiyon mein swasthya sevaen (medical facilities) pahunchane aur logon ko bimariyon se bachane ke liye camps aayojit karti hai.

  • Free Health checkup & consultation camps
  • Zarooratmand logon ko muft dawaiyan
  • Emergency medical financial support

Social Welfare (Kalyan)

Gareeb parivaron ke jeewan star ko sudharne, aapaatkalin sthitiyon (disasters) mein turant rahat aur thand ke mausam mein bachav ke ahem karyakram.

  • Sardiyon mein kambal (blanket) distribution
  • Ration kits distribution (Gareeb parivaron ko)
  • Disaster Relief (Baadh/Aafat ke waqt madad)
Hamare 14 Mukhy Uddeshya

Core Objectives

Zoon Charitable Trust ke 14 pramukh lakshya (goals) jinpar hamari poori team lagatar din-raat kaam karti hai:

01
Samaj Kalyan Social Welfare

Shiksha, health aur gareebi unmulan ke liye samagrah roop se kaam karna.

02
Nishulk Shiksha Free Education

Gareeb aur kamzor bachon ko scholarships aur padhai ke liye financial help dena.

03
Swasthya Seva Healthcare Services

Medical camps lagana, bimariyon se bachav ke liye jagrukta (awareness) failana.

04
Buniyadi Zarooraten Basic Needs Support

Zarooratmand logon ko khana, chath (shelter), aur kapde pradan karna.

05
Samudaayik Kalyan Community Care

Anath, buzurg, divyang aur beghar logon ki dekhbhal aur ashray ka prabandh karna.

06
Paryavaran Sanrakshan Environment Care

Ped lagana (plantation), pradushan kam karna, aur prakriti ki suraksha.

07
Sanskritik Karyakram Cultural Events

Art, music, aur theater ke zariye samajik sandesh failana aur culture badhava dena.

08
Manvadhikar Human Rights

Har aam aur kamzor nagrik ke kanooni aur samajik adhikaaron ki hifazat karna.

09
Aarthik Vikas Rural Development

Gaon dehat mein vikas, sinchai (irrigation), aur bijli/paani ki suvidha.

10
Samajik Nyay Social Justice

Jaati, dharam, ling ya naxl ke aadhar par hone wale bhedbhav ko samaj se mitana.

11
Aapaat Relief Disaster Relief

Baadh, bhookamp aur toofan jaisi aafat ke waqt peediton tak turant rahat pahunchana.

12
Samajik Seva Social Service

Swachhta abhiyan (cleanliness drive) aur blood donation camps lagana.

13
Kanooni Sahayata Free Legal Aid

Gareeb aur peedit logon ko muft kanooni salah aur nayayilay mein madad dena.

14
Shodh & Vikas Research & Dev.

Gareebi door karne aur samaj ko unnat banane ke liye naye tareeqon par shodh.
